The difference

Why people stay on the line

Everyone has been trapped by a bad phone menu. These are the six things that make callers forget they're talking to software.

You can interrupt it

Talk over it and it stops, listens, and picks up from what you actually said. It won't finish its sentence first.

It won't cut you off

A cough, a TV in the background or a pause mid-thought won't make it assume you've finished. It waits for a real gap.

It admits what it is

Ask whether it's a bot and it says yes, warmly, then carries on. It never claims to be a person.

It hangs up on answerphones

Voicemail, switchboards and other companies' AI receptionists are spotted in seconds and dropped — no wasted minutes, no junk leads.

It takes no for an answer

One polite reply to an objection, then a warm goodbye. It will never badger a prospect into making a complaint about you.

It says numbers properly

Prices, postcodes and phone numbers are spoken the way a person says them — read back and confirmed before it moves on.

Built responsibly

The parts that keep you out of trouble

An agent making thousands of calls in your name is a risk if it can improvise. Ours can't. Every limit you set is enforced in software, not left to the model to remember.

Our agents are built to support your calling obligations. Screening your lists against do-not-call registers before dialling remains your responsibility, and we'll help you set that up.

It always says it's an AI

Asked directly, it confirms it. It never pretends to be a person.

Do-not-call is instant

An opt-out stops the call immediately, is flagged in your system, and is never argued with.

Calling hours respected

Local time per region, including multi-timezone markets. It won't book a callback outside a legal window.

It can't improvise

No invented prices, offers, guarantees or claims. It says only what's in your script.

It gives no advice

In regulated industries it gathers information and routes to a licensed human. It never advises.

Every call accounted for

A full transcript and outcome for each call, so you can audit exactly what was said.

Questions

Before you ask us

Will people know it's a bot?

Many won't at first. If they ask, it tells them — always. We don't build agents that pretend to be human.

What if someone asks something off-script?

It answers what it can from what you've given it, and says a human will cover the rest. It never invents an answer.

Can it transfer to my team?

Yes. A qualified lead can be put straight through while they're still on the line, or booked as a callback if nobody's free.

Do I need to change my phone system?

No. It connects to your existing numbers, carrier and dialler.

How many calls can it handle at once?

As many as your phone lines allow. It doesn't queue, and it isn't busier at five o'clock.

What happens to the recordings and data?

It runs on infrastructure you can point at, and call data goes into your systems. Nothing is resold or used to train anything.

Can I change the script after go-live?

Yes, and you should. Most clients tune the opening in the first fortnight once real calls show what's working.

What if it doesn't work for us?

Start with one campaign as a pilot. If the calls aren't good enough, you've lost a week rather than a quarter.

Which languages can it speak?

Hindi and English today, including British, Australian and US accents, with more added on request — usually in days.

How quickly can we start?

Seven days from our first conversation, assuming your script and numbers are ready on day one.
